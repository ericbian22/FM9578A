INCREMENTS ≠ LEVELS

Brownian independence & dependence

$\operatorname{Cov}(W_s,W_t)=\min(s,t)$. For forward increments, covariance equals overlap length: $\max(0,\min(b,d)-\max(a,c))$ for $W_b-W_a$ and $W_d-W_c$. Linear combinations of Brownian levels are jointly Gaussian: zero covariance then implies independence. This shortcut does not apply to arbitrary nonlinear functions.
